# Product: HP Victus 15 gaming laptop with NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5050 (Thanksgiving 2026 campaign)

Sources: Notebookcheck lab review of the Victus 15-fa2160ng (published 27 Oct 2025); LaptopMedia lab review of the
Victus 15 (15-fb3000); FinalBoss RTX 5050 laptop roundup (US starting price); GamesRadar early Black Friday 2026 coverage.
Everything marked ASSUMED is a campaign assumption, not an HP fact. Replace it with HP's real offer before any real use.

## Product facts (verified in the reviews above)
- Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5050 Laptop GPU, 8 GB GDDR7 video memory, 80 W power limit.
- Display: 15.6-inch Full HD (1920 x 1080) IPS, 144 Hz refresh rate, matte (non-glossy).
- Display weakness: color coverage is limited, about 64% sRGB in the Notebookcheck unit and 57% in the LaptopMedia unit.
  Colors look washed out for photo and video editing. Brightness is about 300 nits.
- Independent lab result: 176 FPS average in Counter-Strike 2 at 1080p, Very High settings (LaptopMedia, RTX 5050 unit).
- Upgradeable: two RAM slots (SO-DIMM DDR5) and two M.2 SSD slots. Notebookcheck's unit shipped with 24 GB RAM and a 512 GB SSD,
  with one RAM slot and one SSD slot still free.
- Processor depends on the model: Intel Core i5-13420H in the Notebookcheck unit, AMD Ryzen 5 240 in the LaptopMedia unit.
  LaptopMedia found its processor runs very hot under sustained load (up to 100 C); Notebookcheck called its CPU somewhat outdated.
- Cooling and noise: LaptopMedia found stable GPU performance with excellent GPU thermal management; Notebookcheck found moderate noise and temperatures under load.
- Build: plastic case that can rattle in places; weight about 2.4 kg (5.2 lb); 200 W power adapter.
- Ports: HDMI 2.1, USB-C (DisplayPort 1.4 and power delivery), two USB-A 3.0, RJ45 Ethernet, audio jack.
- Missing: no USB4, no SD card reader. Keyboard has a full numpad but cramped half-height arrow keys.
- Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi 6 class (Realtek RTL8852BE) in the Notebookcheck unit.
- Warranty: 12 months in Germany (Notebookcheck). US warranty terms are not confirmed here.
- Battery life is modest for a gaming laptop: about 5 h 44 min in LaptopMedia's battery test; a 2025 RTX 5060 Victus 15 review measured 4 h 27 min.
  Gaming on battery lasts far less than that.
- Does NOT have an OLED screen, a high-refresh 240 Hz panel, a high-wattage RTX 5060 or 5070 GPU, or a metal chassis.
- It is the lower-priced Victus line from HP. HP's premium gaming line is OMEN, listed from about $1,299 (GamesRadar, 2026) and going much higher.

## Price and market context
- Street price range found in research: from about $699 (FinalBoss, US, 2026 Victus 15 with RTX 5050 as the cheapest RTX 5050 laptop it tracks)
  up to about $1,200 (Notebookcheck, Oct 2025, for the reviewed configuration).
- GamesRadar (early Black Friday 2026 coverage) reports gaming laptops are about 38% more expensive than at Black Friday 2025 and
  expects smaller discounts than usual. This is a secondary source; treat it as context, not an HP claim.
- ASSUMED Thanksgiving offer for this campaign: $799 for the Victus 15 with RTX 5050, offered Thursday 26 Nov 2026
  (Thanksgiving) through Monday 30 Nov 2026 (Cyber Monday). Not an HP-announced price.
- No claim is made about shipping speed, stock levels, financing, free games, or trade-in value.

## Target audience (hypothesis)
Shoppers who plan to buy a gaming laptop during Thanksgiving week: students, parents buying a gift for a teenager,
competitive players on a budget, and people who want one machine for work by day and games at night.
