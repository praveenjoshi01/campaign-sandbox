# Product: PantryPilot (SAMPLE — replace with your real product)

PantryPilot is a $9/month app that turns the groceries you already own into a weekly dinner plan.
You scan your fridge and pantry once; it suggests 5 dinners that use what you have, and builds a
short shopping list for only what is missing.

## Facts
- Price: $9/month, first 14 days free, cancel any time.
- Works with a phone camera scan; no barcode typing.
- Average user plans 5 dinners a week.
- Beta users report throwing away less food (self-reported, 312 beta users, 3 months).
- Self-reported result: beta users estimate they threw away about 31% less food by week 12 (ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE NUMBER - replace with real data before any real campaign; survey, not an audited study).
- Recipes are 30 minutes or less. Dietary filters: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free.
- Does NOT order groceries or deliver anything. It only plans and lists.
- Founded by two ex-grocery-chain engineers. Company is 14 months old. No big-brand backing.

## Target audience (hypothesis)
Busy households that buy groceries weekly and feel guilty about wasted food.
